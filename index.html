About Vanja Moves
Australian movement philosopher and mobility expert (@vanja.moves on Instagram & YouTube). Philosophy: train MOVEMENT not just muscles. Her Making Moves podcast and MovesMethod program (~$27) are excellent companions to this plan.

Movement finisher — after every gym session (her)

His spine-safe Vanja moves
Adapted for post-discectomy recovery. The passive hang decompresses the spine — excellent for disc recovery. Stop if any nerve pain.
Daily challenge — every day, both of you
Squat & hang challenge
Accumulate 10 minutes of passive resting squat throughout the day. Accumulate 1 minute of passive hang from any bar. Break into small doses — TV, teeth brushing, cooking. Track it in the Tracker tab. Life-changing over 8 weeks.
Run day progression
Her run plan
Wks 1–2: 20–25 min easy jog. Wks 3–4: 25–30 min + 2x 2-min faster intervals. Wks 5–6: 30 min + 3x 3-min intervals. Wks 7–8: 30–35 min non-stop.
His run plan
Wks 1–4: Brisk walk or easy 20 min jog, no sprinting. Wks 5–8: Easy 25–30 min jog. Alternative: stationary bike or cross trainer — great for disc recovery.
His recovery reminder
Get surgeon/physio clearance before starting. No deadlifts, no barbell squats, no overhead barbell press, no box jumps. Stop if sharp pain, radiating leg pain, numbness or tingling occurs.
